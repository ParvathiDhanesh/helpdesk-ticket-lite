IT HELPDESK FAQ

How do I reset my VPN password?
1. Open the VPN app and click "Forgot password".
2. Enter your work email and click Send code.
3. Enter the 6-digit code and choose a new password.
4. Restart the VPN app and sign in.
If you do not receive the code within 10 minutes, raise a ticket under the Access category.

My Wi-Fi is not working. What should I do?
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
2. Forget the office network and reconnect.
3. Restart your laptop.
If other people are also affected, raise a Network ticket with High priority.

My laptop will not start.
1. Plug in the charger and wait 10 minutes.
2. Hold the power button for 15 seconds, then press it once.
3. Remove any USB devices and try again.
If it still does not start, raise a Hardware ticket.

How do I reset my email password?
1. Go to the company sign-in page and click "Forgot my password".
2. Verify with your phone and set a new password.
If you cannot verify, raise an Access ticket.

My printer is not connecting.
1. Check that the printer is on and connected to the same network.
2. Remove and re-add the printer in your settings.
If it still fails, raise a Hardware ticket with Low priority.
